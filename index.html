03 / Working together

A small, clear first step.

Whether the need is an urgent fix or ongoing maintenance, start with a specific result both sides can check.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Share the problem.

Send the platform, store URL or screenshot, expected result and timeline. Keep credentials and customer data out of the first message.

02 / DEFINE

Agree on scope.

Clarify likely work, access, acceptance checks and whether a fixed quote or hourly work fits better.

03 / DELIVER

Make it maintainable.

The handoff states what changed, what was checked and any remaining risks. Ongoing support can follow the first task.
